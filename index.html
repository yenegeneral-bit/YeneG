About Yene General LLC

The general store, done right

Yene General LLC — YeneG for short — is a general store built around a simple idea: the things your household needs every week should be easy to find, good quality, and fairly priced, all in one place.

From the pantry to the laundry room, the kitchen to the closet, our shelves carry the general merchandise daily life runs on — so your list gets shorter and your tomorrow gets better.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What does YeneG sell?

General merchandise across six departments: groceries & pantry staples, home & kitchen goods, clothing & apparel, beauty & personal care, cleaning & household supplies, and electronics & accessories.

What does “YeneG” stand for?

YeneG is short for Yene General LLC — you’ll see the YG monogram on our logo, along with our promise: Quality Products For A Better Tomorrow.

How does the shopping list on this page work?

Add essentials to My List as you browse, adjust quantities in the list panel, then copy the finished list to take with you. It’s kept on this page only, so build it in one visit.

I can’t find something on the shelves — can you help?

Yes — ask any of us in store. If it’s a general-merchandise item we don’t currently carry, tell us: customer requests shape what we stock next.
